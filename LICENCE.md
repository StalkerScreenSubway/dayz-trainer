## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- ESP — see players, zombies, and items through walls
- Aimbot — auto-aim at enemies with adjustable smoothing
- Speedhack — increase movement speed (1.0x to 10x)
- No Recoil — remove weapon recoil entirely
- Infinite Stamina — never run out of stamina

### 🛠️ Spawning & Teleport
- Item Spawner — spawn any item into inventory
- Vehicle Spawner — spawn vehicles anywhere
- Teleport to Waypoint — instantly travel to map markers
- Teleport to Player — teleport to selected player

### 🔫 Combat & Survival
- God Mode — take no damage from players or zombies

- No Hunger/Thirst — keep stats always full
- No Diseases — immune to all infections
